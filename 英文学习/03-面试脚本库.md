# 面试脚本库（背这个就够了）

> 外企技术面 80% 的问题可预测。**不要临场翻译中文，要背结构 + 关键词。**
> 使用方法：每张卡只背 **5 个关键词**，看着关键词复述 2 分钟，反复到脱口而出。

---

## 一、自我介绍（90 秒，必须背到肌肉记忆）

### 结构：Now → Past → Why

```
Hi, I'm [Name]. I'm a [backend / full-stack] engineer with [12] years of experience.

Currently I work at [Company], where I'm responsible for [一句话职责].
My main focus is [核心技术领域: distributed systems / payment platform / data pipeline].

Before that, I worked at [Company2] as a [title], building [什么东西].
And I started my career at [Company3] doing [什么].

I'd say my strength is [一个差异化优势: turning ambiguous requirements into shipped systems].
Recently I led a project where [一句话最有成就感的成果 + 数字].

The reason I'm interested in this role is [公司/团队/技术方向的具体理由].

That's a quick intro. Happy to dive into anything.
```

### 填空示范（把 [] 换成你的）

```
Hi, I'm Wei. I'm a backend engineer with 12 years of experience.

Currently I work at a fintech company, where I lead the payment platform team.
My main focus is high-throughput distributed systems.

Before that, I spent four years at an e-commerce company, building the order service
that handles about 50,000 requests per second at peak.

I'd say my strength is turning vague business requirements into systems that ship.
Last year I led a migration that cut our p99 latency from 800ms to 120ms.

The reason I'm interested in this role is that your team owns the core trading
infrastructure, and that's exactly the kind of problem I want to go deeper on.

That's a quick intro. Happy to dive into anything.
```

> **要点**：
> - 控制在 90 秒，别超过 2 分钟
> - 必须有 **1–2 个数字**（QPS、延迟、成本、团队规模）
> - 最后一句"Happy to dive into anything"是**主动交棒**，把话语权给面试官

## 二、12 张故事卡（覆盖 90% 行为面问题）

### 故事卡模板

```
【标题】：
【场景 Situation】：什么背景，为什么重要（1–2 句）
【任务 Task】：我的具体职责是什么（1 句）
【行动 Action】：我做了什么，3 个要点（重点！占 60% 篇幅）
【结果 Result】：量化结果（必须有数字）
【反思 Learning】：如果重来我会怎么做
【关键词】：5 个词，用于现场提示
```

### 必须准备的 12 张卡

| # | 卡片主题 | 对应高频问题 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 最有挑战的项目 | Tell me about your most challenging project |
| 2 | 技术攻坚/难题 | Tell me about a hard technical problem you solved |
| 3 | 线上故障处理 | Tell me about a production incident |
| 4 | 性能优化 | Tell me about a time you improved performance |
| 5 | 从 0 到 1 建系统 | Tell me about a project you built from scratch |
| 6 | 技术选型/架构决策 | Tell me about a technical decision and its trade-offs |
| 7 | 与同事冲突 | Tell me about a conflict with a coworker |
| 8 | 说服他人/推动方案 | Tell me about a time you convinced others |
| 9 | 失败/犯错 | Tell me about a failure |
| 10 | 带新人/技术领导力 | Tell me about your leadership experience |
| 11 | 跨部门协作 | Tell me about cross-team collaboration |
| 12 | 紧急排期/取舍 | Tell me about a time you had to cut scope |

### 故事卡示范（卡 3：线上故障）

```
【标题】Payment service outage during flash sale

【Situation】Last year during a flash sale, our payment service started timing out.
Error rate jumped from 0.1% to 30% within five minutes.

【Task】I was the on-call engineer and the tech lead for the payment platform.

【Action】
First, I focused on stopping the bleeding — I rolled back the most recent deploy
within four minutes, which brought errors down but not to zero.
Second, I pulled the metrics and found the connection pool was exhausted:
a new query was holding connections for 10 seconds instead of 50 milliseconds.
Third, I added a circuit breaker and a query timeout, then shipped a proper fix
the next day with a load test that reproduced the issue.

【Result】Recovery in 12 minutes total. We also wrote a postmortem and added
connection-pool alerting, and we haven't had a similar incident since.

【Learning】If I did it again, I'd add the timeout at the ORM level from day one,
not just at the application level.

【关键词】flash sale / rollback / connection pool / circuit breaker / postmortem
```

> **每张卡都这样写**，然后只背关键词。现场看着关键词讲，不要背逐字稿——背稿会一被打断就崩。

## 三、高频问题清单 + 答题框架

### 技术类

| 问题 | 框架 | 时长 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk me through your resume | Now → Past → Why | 90s |
| Tell me about your current project | What → Why → How → Impact | 2–3 min |
| What's the most complex system you've built? | Context → Constraints → Design → Trade-offs → Result | 3 min |
| How do you approach system design? | Requirements → Scale → API → Data → Scale-out → Failure | 5 min |
| How do you ensure code quality? | Review / Test / CI / Observability 四件套 | 1 min |
| How do you keep learning? | 具体例子，别说"看博客" | 1 min |

### 行为类

| 问题 | 框架 |
| --- | --- |
| Tell me about a conflict | 先讲**分歧是技术性的**，再讲你怎么用数据说服 |
| Tell me about a failure | 讲真失败 + 你学到的 + 后续怎么改 |
| Why are you leaving? | **绝不抱怨**，讲"想成长/想做更大的事" |
| Why this company? | 讲**具体**的技术/产品理由，别说"公司很有名" |
| Where do you see yourself in 5 years? | 讲深度（技术专家）而不是"当经理" |
| What's your weakness? | 讲真弱点 + 你在怎么改进 |
| How do you handle pressure? | 讲优先级排序 + 沟通，不要讲"我能熬夜" |

### 反问环节（必须准备 3 个，且要有质量）

✅ 好的反问：
- How does the team measure success for this role in the first six months?
- What's the biggest technical challenge the team is facing right now?
- How do you balance shipping fast with paying down technical debt?
- How is the on-call rotation structured?
- What does the code review culture look like here?

❌ 坏的反问（第一轮别问）：
- 薪资多少？（留到 HR 轮）
- 加班多吗？（换成 "How does the team handle crunch time?"）
- 我能远程吗？（留到 offer 阶段谈）

## 四、系统设计白板沟通模板

> 系统设计面试考的是**沟通**，不是答案。用这套句式把过程说出来。

```
Step 1 - 澄清需求
"Before I dive in, let me clarify a few things.
Who are the users, and what's the expected scale?
Is this read-heavy or write-heavy?
What's more important here — consistency or availability?"

Step 2 - 给规模估算
"Let me do a quick back-of-the-envelope calculation.
If we have 10 million daily active users and each makes 20 requests,
that's about 200 million requests per day, so roughly 2,300 QPS on average,
and maybe 3 to 5 times that at peak."

Step 3 - 高层设计
"At a high level, I'd start with a client, a load balancer, an API layer,
a service layer, and a data layer. Let me draw that out."

Step 4 - 深挖
"Now let me zoom into the data layer.
The key decision here is SQL versus NoSQL. I'd lean toward SQL because..."

Step 5 - 权衡
"The trade-off is between consistency and latency.
If we choose strong consistency, we pay with higher write latency.
Given the requirement, I'd choose eventual consistency here."

Step 6 - 故障与扩展
"Let me talk about failure modes.
What happens if this cache goes down? We'd fall back to the database,
which would probably be overloaded, so I'd add a circuit breaker and a queue."

Step 7 - 收尾
"That's my design. The main bottleneck would be the write path,
and the next thing I'd optimize is sharding the orders table."
```

**救场句**：想不出时不要说 "I don't know"，说：
> "Let me think out loud for a second." / "I'd want to check the numbers, but my instinct is..."

## 五、谈薪与收尾

| 场景 | 说法 |
| --- | --- |
| 被问期望薪资 | "I'd like to learn more about the scope first. What's the range for this level?" |
| 给出数字 | "Based on my experience and the market, I'm targeting [X] to [Y]." |
| 被压价 | "I'm flexible on the structure. Could we look at the total package?" |
| 需要时间考虑 | "Thank you. Could I have a couple of days to review the details?" |
| 结束语 | "Thanks for your time today. I really enjoyed the conversation about [具体话题]." |

## 六、面试前一天 Checklist

- [ ] 自我介绍 3 遍，录音确认 ≤2 分钟
- [ ] 12 张故事卡的关键词过一遍
- [ ] 3 个反问准备好
- [ ] 目标公司：读一遍官网 + 工程博客 + 最近融资/产品新闻
- [ ] 技术栈：把 JD 里出现的关键词过一遍英文表达
- [ ] 设备测试：摄像头、麦克风、网络、备用热点
- [ ] 环境：安静房间、光线、水、纸笔
- [ ] 心理：**目标不是全对，是让面试官觉得"能一起工作"**
